The BERT-based Resume Screening System (BRSS) uses a fine-tuned BERT model to analyze resumes and rank job candidates based on relevance to job descriptions. This case study evaluates BRSS using NIST AI RMF, EU AI Act and ISO 42001 governance principles. 

The assessment concludes that BRSS qualifies as a High-Risk AI System under the EU AI Act due to its impact on employment access, fairness, and discrimination risk.
